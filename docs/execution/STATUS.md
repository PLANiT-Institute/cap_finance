# CAP-KJ execution status

## Fixed objective

Produce company-level capital-allocation pathways from facility-level evidence so investors and companies can see transition timing, required capital, cost gaps, support dependence, modelled abatement, physical constraints, and residual common exposure.

## Operating rules

- One bounded role per 30-minute execution: data, model, investor output, or QA.
- Produce a minimal usable output before adding sophistication.
- Prefer official observed data. Estimates are allowed only with `value_type`, source or rationale, formula, unit, price year, low/base/high range, and quality flag.
- Never silently convert company totals into facility facts.
- Every company headline must disclose facility coverage.
- Do not treat a closure as system abatement before replacement production and leakage are considered.
- Do not estimate company valuation, security returns, or a basis-point risk premium in Stage 1.
